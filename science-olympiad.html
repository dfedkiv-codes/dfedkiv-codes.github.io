How I write them

An exam’s job is to separate teams, so every question has to earn its place by telling two levels of preparation apart. I calibrate difficulty across the whole paper instead of question by question, and I build wrong answers out of the specific mistakes people make, so a distractor is only tempting if you made that mistake.

The questions I like most are the multi-part ones where chemistry you already know has to be pushed somewhere new: kinetics that don’t sit still, a mechanism you have to time, a gas problem with one more constraint than is comfortable.

Everything is typeset in LaTeX, because competitors deserve an exam that looks like someone cared.
