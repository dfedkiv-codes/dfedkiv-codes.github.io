Fields Lab

until 2026

Florida Atlantic University, Jupiter, Florida

Matrix metalloproteinases. I made FRET probes for MMP-13 and worked on inhibitors of MT1-MMP, running the peptides from Fmoc synthesis through purification and into fluorescence kinetics assays.

Fmoc SPPS, HPLC, UPLC-MS, MALDI, enzyme kinetics in 384-well plates

What I can do at the bench and after it

  • Synthesis. Fmoc solid-phase peptide synthesis, cleavage, and purification by HPLC.
  • Characterization. UPLC-MS and MALDI to confirm that what I made is what I meant to make.
  • Enzymology. FRET substrate assays, progress curves across multiple timepoints, and IC50 values from four-parameter logistic fits.
  • Analysis. My own Python tools for turning plate reader exports into fitted curves.
