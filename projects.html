Biomedical data science

A 20-week curriculum, taught to myself

A course I designed to get from clinical data analysis to cheminformatics and graph neural networks, with a capstone predicting 30-day hospital readmission. The work lives in three repositories:

  • biomedds-MIMIC, analyses on the MIMIC critical care database
  • biomedds-NHANES, analyses on the NHANES national health survey
  • biomedds-misc, everything that fit neither
